02 / METHOD
01

Protein-only input

A protein chain with matching sequence and Cα coordinates. Nucleic-acid coordinates and query binding labels are not inputs.

02

Frozen SSCP ensemble

SaProt and structure-aware ESM3 representations feed model C. The five fold probabilities—not logits—are averaged.

03

Remote interface memory

Qualified structural alignments project training-memory interface evidence. Uncovered residues retain the base prediction within the frozen clipping convention.

04

Task-specific fusion

DNA uses the frozen count-based rule. RNA uses CR: effective template count is one when a residue is covered.

BEFORE YOU SUBMIT

Know what the scores mean.

RIMBind estimates which protein residues may participate in DNA or RNA binding. A probability-colored surface is a prediction, not an experimentally established interface.

Read the prediction tutorial and limitations ↗
Where do I run a new prediction?

Use Open GPU predictor at the top of this page. The external Space runs the real model; this GitHub Pages workspace remains a local explorer. Both task workflows passed fixed-case numerical and downloaded-file checks. Broader operational acceptance remains in progress; a Space restart may require owner revalidation.

What do I upload?

A PDB protein structure of at most 2 MiB, with one selected alphanumeric chain and at most 600 observed standard amino acids, each with a Cα coordinate. Enter the original chain ID, select DNA or RNA, and optionally provide the exact observed sequence and original four-character PDB accession. Only the first model is used. Sequence-only prediction and automatic ColabFold/AlphaFold generation are not offered.

What happens to my files?

Local inspection and CSV visualization stay in your browser. Selecting the external link does not upload a file. Submitting in the Space sends your structure to Hugging Face infrastructure after consent. App results and Gradio caches are eligible for cleanup after one hour, checked every five minutes while running; this is not a platform-wide deletion guarantee. Download promptly. Do not submit confidential, personal or sensitive unpublished data.

Why might the predictor be unavailable?

ZeroGPU is shared and has per-user quotas, queues and interruptions. Free access is not unlimited compute or an uptime guarantee. If a quota error appears, wait for the platform-reported reset rather than submitting repeatedly. If the predictor awaits acceptance after a restart, contact the repository maintainer through GitHub Issues without sharing data or credentials. See ZeroGPU documentation.

Why no performance number for my query?

AP, AUROC, F1 and MCC require experimental labels. An unlabeled query has no such reference. A high score or a memory-driven change alone is not measured accuracy. PDB B-fields contain probability on a 0–1 scale, not pLDDT or experimental temperature factors; CSV keeps higher precision.

How should I cite this website?

The manuscript citation and permanent model-asset release are pending. Link to the repository and record the deployment/source version for reproducibility. Research-beta availability is not journal certification or proof of model accuracy.
